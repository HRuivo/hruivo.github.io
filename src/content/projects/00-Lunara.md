Language and compiler written in Zig for flight-mission firmware, inspired by NASA/JPL's Power of Ten rules. Features unit-aware types, checked arithmetic, mandatory wait timeouts, and fault handling. Compiles to bare-metal RV32I with no heap or recursion. Includes mission scenarios, fault injection, and waveform reports for testing flight logic.
